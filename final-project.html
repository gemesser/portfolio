EP & Audio Technology

A multidisciplinary final university project combining music production with audio technology. The project centred around the production and release of an EP while exploring modular synthesis, signal flow, recording, Max/MSP, audio-reactive systems and electronic audio hardware.

01 — OVERVIEW

Music production meets audio technology.

My final university project brought together the creative and technical areas developed throughout my Audio Engineering and Sound Production degree.

The central outcome was an EP developed through recording, production, mixing and mastering. Alongside the music, the project explored the technical systems behind audio production, including modular synthesis, patch-bay routing, signal flow, Max/MSP systems and audio electronics.

03 — PRODUCTION

From recording to release.

The EP required the complete production process, combining creative decisions with the technical workflow required to produce finished music for commercial release.

Recording

Recorded musical material including guitar while applying practical microphone, gain staging and signal-flow techniques developed through audio engineering.

04 — AUDIO SYSTEMS

Signal flow & modular synthesis.

The project extended beyond producing the final music by exploring how audio signals are generated, routed and processed through hardware systems.

Modular Synthesis

Worked with modular synthesizer systems to explore synthesis, signal generation, modulation and audio routing.

Patch Bays

Developed practical understanding of patch-bay routing and how signals move between different parts of an audio production system.

Signal Flow

Applied signal-flow principles across recording, synthesis and production workflows to understand how audio moves through interconnected hardware and software.

05 — MAX/MSP

Creative audio programming.

Max/MSP was used to explore programmable approaches to audio, moving beyond conventional DAW workflows into custom audio and interactive systems.

Drum Machine

Designed a drum-machine system in Max/MSP, exploring sequencing, audio playback and the construction of a custom software-based musical tool.

Audio-Reactive Visuals

Mapped recorded audio to visual systems within Max/MSP, using characteristics of the audio to influence visual behaviour.

Interactive Systems

Explored the relationship between audio, control data and interactive media through patch-based programming.

06 — AUDIO ELECTRONICS

Understanding the hardware.

The technical component also included practical work with electronic audio hardware, connecting software and music production with the physical systems that generate and process audio.

HARDWARE

Soldering, Modules & PCB Fundamentals

Worked with modular audio hardware and soldering while developing an understanding of modules, electronic components and printed circuit boards used within audio systems.

07 — PROJECT OUTCOME

Connecting creative and technical audio.

The project brought together music production, recording, software, synthesis and electronics rather than treating them as separate disciplines.

Producing the EP provided the creative output, while work with Max/MSP, modular synthesis, routing and electronics developed a deeper understanding of the technology and signal processing systems behind audio production.
